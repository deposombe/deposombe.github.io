Rapidfire Help

Keyboard Shortcuts

Every key Rapidfire uses, on one page. The menu bar icon’s menu opens this page directly: Control-click the icon and choose Keyboard Shortcuts.

Last updated 22 November 2026

From Anywhere In the Overlay: Picking In the Overlay: Views In the Overlay: Input Fields With the Pointer in the Overlay Quick Add The Editor Every Window

From Anywhere

KeysWhat they do
A palette’s hotkeyOpens that palette’s overlay. Pressed again, closes it. Hotkeys are recorded in Settings > Hotkeys; a new installation has Control-Option-Space for General.
Click the menu bar iconOpens the General palette.
Control-click the menu bar iconShows Rapidfire’s menu.
Drag onto the menu bar iconOpens Quick Add for a link, a file, a folder or an app.

In the Overlay: Picking

KeysWhat they do
Letters, digits, SpaceNarrow the list. The trigger opens when it is the only one left.
DeleteTakes back the last key. With nothing typed, goes back out of a folder.
↑ ↓ ← →Move the highlight.
ReturnOpens the highlighted row.
TabOpens the most used trigger in the list.
⌃ (held)The trigger picked opens its whole palette.
⌃TabOpens the whole palette of the most used trigger.
EscapeCloses the overlay.

In the Overlay: Views

KeysWhat they do
⌘TShow All: opens every folder in place, until pressed again.
⌥ (held)Shows every folder opened while held.
⌘KWatching: keeps the overlay open, redraws it when the library changes, and lets it be moved by its top row.
⌘]The next pointer style.
⌘[The previous pointer style.
⌘1Left hand.
⌘2Both hands.
⌘3Right hand.
⌘+Larger text.
⌘−Smaller text.
⌘0Default text size.
⌘EOpens the editor.
⌘,Opens Settings.
⌘WCloses the overlay.

In the Overlay: Input Fields

KeysWhat they do
TabMoves to the next field.
ReturnOpens the trigger with what was typed.
DeleteIn the empty first field, goes back to the list.
EscapeCancels.

With the Pointer in the Overlay

KeysWhat they do
Click a rowOpens it.
⌃-click a rowOpens its whole palette.
Drag a row outGives the link that shows the trigger or opens the palette.
⌥-drag a row outGives the link that runs it.

Quick Add

KeysWhat they do
ReturnAdds the trigger.
EscapeCancels.
⌘.Cancels.
⌘1 to ⌘9Choose one of the palettes listed.
⌘0Chooses General.
↓In the palette field, opens the list of palettes.

The Editor

KeysWhat they do
⌘NNew Trigger.
⇧⌘NNew Palette.
⌥⇧⌘NNew Dynamic Palette.
⌘OOpens the selected triggers.
⌘FFind.
⌘CCopies the selected triggers as links.
⌘VPastes links, files or triggers into the palette.
Delete or ⌘DeleteDeletes the selection, after asking.
⌘ZUndo.
⇧⌘ZRedo.
⌥⌘↑Move Palette Up.
⌥⌘↓Move Palette Down.
⌃⌘SShows or hides the sidebar.
⌥⌘IShows or hides Palette Settings.

Every Window

KeysWhat they do
⌘EOpens the editor.
⌘,Opens Settings.
⌘?Opens Rapidfire Help.
⌘WCloses the window.

Rapidfire has no ⌘Q. Quitting would stop every hotkey, so it is done on purpose: choose Quit Rapidfire from the Rapidfire menu or from the menu bar icon’s menu.
