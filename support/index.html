Support

Write to support@deposombe.com.

From Rapidfire 1.0.5, the quickest way is Help › Report a Problem in the app (also in its menu bar icon's menu): it writes the email with the versions filled in. After a crash, Rapidfire offers the same the next time it opens.

Writing by hand, helpful to include:

  • the app and its version (About Rapidfire) and the macOS version,
  • what was done, what was expected, and what happened instead,
  • a screenshot or a short screen recording, if it shows the problem,
  • for sync problems: Settings › Sync › Copy Sync Report, pasted into the mail.

Replies come within 48 hours.

Rapidfire

Rapidfire 1.0 was released on 21 November 2026. Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later.

Download Rapidfire Help Keyboard shortcuts Troubleshooting

Known issues

  • macOS 15.0 and 15.1 refuse hotkeys that use only Option, or only Option and Shift. macOS 15.2 and later accept them.
  • A trigger for a file opens only when the file's folder is listed in Settings › Files.

How to install and update?

Open the disk image and drag Rapidfire to the Applications folder, then open it from there. Opened from the disk image or from the Downloads folder it cannot update itself; its menu then starts with Move Rapidfire to the Applications Folder…. Once installed, Rapidfire checks for a new version once a day; Check for Updates… in the menu bar icon's menu asks at once.

Where are the triggers stored?

In one file, library.json, with a daily copy in the Backups folder next to it. Show Backups in Finder, in the menu bar icon's menu, opens that folder. File › Export Library… saves a copy anywhere.

How to go back to a backup?

File › Restore Library… and choose a file from Backups or an exported copy.

What works without a license?

The General palette, complete and without limits: any number of triggers, Quick Add, the editor, Spotlight and Siri, Shortcuts, links, iCloud sync and the MCP server. A license adds more palettes, their hotkeys and palettes inside palettes. Without one these show a padlock; nothing is hidden and there are no reminders.

What happens to palettes when a license is removed?

They are locked, not deleted: listed with a padlock, kept in the library, in backups and in iCloud sync, and working again once a license is entered. A locked palette can still be deleted, and Export Library and Restore Library work as always.

Does Rapidfire need to be online?

Only to activate a key and to remove a license. After that it confirms the license about once a day when it can, and works for 30 days without a connection. After 30 days without one, palettes besides General lock until the Mac is online again; nothing has to be entered again.

A hotkey does nothing.

See Troubleshooting in Rapidfire Help. On macOS 15.0 and 15.1 the system refuses hotkeys that use only Option, or only Option and Shift; macOS 15.2 and later accept them.

How to remove Rapidfire and all its data?

  1. Quit Rapidfire and move it from Applications to the Trash.
  2. In Finder choose Go › Go to Folder…, enter ~/Library/Containers/, and move the com.deposombe.RapidfireLauncher folder to the Trash. This removes triggers, settings and backups.
  3. If iCloud sync was on, Rapidfire's data in iCloud is removed in System Settings, under the Apple Account's iCloud storage, in the list of apps.

A license stays valid after removal. Use Remove License (Settings › License) first to free its place for another Mac.

Licenses and refunds

  • Lost license key: it is in the email from Polar and in the Polar customer portal, under the email used to pay. Or write to support@deposombe.com from that email.
  • New Mac: one license works on 3 Macs. Remove License on a Mac no longer in use frees its place (that Mac must be online); so does the customer portal, where each Mac is listed under the name Settings › License shows as This Mac. Write to support@deposombe.com if that Mac is gone.
  • After moving to a new Mac with Migration Assistant or a backup, Rapidfire says “No license”: a license is tied to the Mac it was activated on and does not move with the data. Enter the key again on the new Mac (it takes one of the 3 places), and free the old Mac's place with Remove License there or in the customer portal. The same after a repair that replaced the Mac's logic board.
  • “This license is already in use on 3 Macs”: free a place as above, then enter the key again.
  • “License not valid”: the purchase was refunded, or this Mac was removed in the customer portal. Remove License, then enter the key again.
  • “License not confirmed”, or the menu line “License Server Not Reached”: the Mac has not reached the license server (api.polar.sh) for 30 days, or for more than three weeks. Usually a firewall or a network filter blocks it: allow Rapidfire to connect to api.polar.sh, then Check Again in Settings › License. Nothing has to be entered again.
  • “License not readable”: the Keychain did not hand over the license. Rapidfire reads it again by itself; Check Again reads it at once. Nothing has to be entered again, and no place is used up.
  • A palette's hotkey opens Settings › License: the license on this Mac does not count at the moment; the pane says why.
  • A place is taken by a Mac that never got licensed (the connection dropped while the key was being activated): free it in the customer portal, or write to support@deposombe.com.
  • Who sells the license: Polar (Polar Software, Inc.), the reseller and merchant of record. Polar takes the payment, sends the license key by email at once, and handles tax, receipts and invoices. Deposombe makes Rapidfire and answers questions about it.
  • Refunds: write to support@deposombe.com from the email used to pay, within 14 days of the purchase. No reason needed. Refunds are paid by Polar to the original payment method.
  • Invoices: Polar sends the receipt and issues an invoice with company details from the customer portal.
