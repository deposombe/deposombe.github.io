This website

deposombe.com sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites: no analytics, trackers, embedded media or third-party fonts. If you switch between light and dark, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes. Deposombe has no access to these logs, and GitHub decides how long it keeps them. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Legal basis: legitimate interests in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Email to Deposombe

Mail to support@deposombe.com is forwarded by Porkbun (USA, the domain's registrar) to a Gmail mailbox (Google). Your message, address and attachments are used only to reply to you. Retention: kept as long as the conversation needs them, then deleted, or sooner if you ask. Writing is voluntary; without an email address there is no way to reply. Legal basis: legitimate interests in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Rapidfire

What Rapidfire 1.0 and later do.

What stays on the Mac

  • Palettes, triggers, hotkeys and settings are files on the Mac, in Rapidfire's own folder, with a daily copy of the library next to them.
  • Usage counts (how often each trigger was opened), backups and the record of what an AI assistant changed stay on the Mac and are deleted after 30 days. They are never synced.
  • Rapidfire has no account. None of this is sent to Deposombe.
  • No analytics and no advertising. Rapidfire shares no data with third parties.

Crash and problem reports

  • From Rapidfire 1.0.5. If Rapidfire quits unexpectedly, macOS gives it a technical record of the crash the next time it opens. Rapidfire keeps that record on the Mac for 30 days and offers to send a report. Settings › General › “Offer to send a report after a crash” turns this off; then no records are kept.
  • Records of slowdowns (Rapidfire not responding for a moment) are kept the same way, for 30 days. They are never offered on their own: they go only into a problem report you send, while “Include the technical records” is ticked there.
  • Nothing is sent unless you choose Send Report and then send the email from your own mail app, or copy the report into an email yourself. Rapidfire opens no connection for it: the report travels as your email to support@deposombe.com, and is handled like other support mail (above).
  • A report contains the versions of Rapidfire and macOS, the Mac's model and processor type, the time and technical details of the crash (the exception, the signal, and the program files involved with their positions), and anything you write in it. Home folder paths are shortened and your account name is removed. It never contains triggers, palettes, links, file names from your library, clipboard contents, the license key or anything typed in the overlay. You can read the whole report before sending it.
  • Help › Report a Problem opens the same window for any other problem.

iCloud sync

  • Sync is off until it is turned on. With sync on, the library and the synced settings are stored in your own iCloud account, in Rapidfire's private iCloud folder. Deposombe cannot see them. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • Backups, usage counts and the AI activity record are not synced.

AI assistants (MCP)

  • Rapidfire's MCP server accepts connections only from programs on the same Mac, with a token kept in the Keychain. It cannot be reached from the internet.
  • A connected assistant can read and change triggers and palettes. What that assistant sends to its own provider is between you and that provider.
  • Trigger lists leave links out unless the assistant asks for them.

Files and folders

  • A trigger that opens a file needs access to its folder. You give that access, per folder or file, and it is listed in Settings › Files. It is kept per Mac and is not synced.
  • Rapidfire opens only what triggers point to. It does not list, read or upload files in the folders it has access to.

License

  • Without a license key, nothing is sent to Polar.
  • When a license key is entered, Rapidfire sends it to Polar (Polar Software, Inc., USA) to activate it, together with a name for that Mac made of the word “Mac” and a random number (never the Mac's own name), and Rapidfire's identifiers at Polar. About once a day while it runs, it asks Polar whether the key still holds, sending the key, the ID Polar gave that Mac's activation, and the same identifiers. No trigger, palette or file name is sent.
  • Like every web request, these carry the IP address and the app's name and version.
  • The key, the activation's ID, the Mac's random name and the date of the last confirmation are stored in the Mac's Keychain. A confirmation counts for 30 days without a connection.
  • The stored license is tied to the Mac it was activated on: Rapidfire keeps a check value with it, computed on the Mac from the stored license and the Mac's hardware identifier. The identifier itself is not stored and is never sent anywhere.
  • Remove License deactivates that Mac at Polar and deletes the key from the Keychain.
  • Kept by Polar with the license: the key, each Mac's random name and the time of activation. Legal basis: the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Updates

  • Once a day Rapidfire asks deposombe.com whether a newer version exists, and downloads an update from GitHub when you confirm it. The daily check is on by default and can be turned off in Settings › General; Check for Updates… asks at once.
  • These requests carry the app's name and version and the updater's own version and, like every web request, the IP address. Nothing about the Mac or the library is sent.
  • Updates are signed; a download that does not carry Deposombe's signature is not installed.
  • Deposombe has no access to the hosts' logs and keeps no record of update checks. Legal basis: legitimate interests in delivering updates and security fixes (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Purchases

Rapidfire is sold by Polar (Polar Software, Inc., USA), the merchant of record. Polar collects payment details, billing address and email, handles tax and invoices, and is responsible for that data under the Polar privacy policy. Deposombe never sees card details.

Polar shares with Deposombe: name and email if given, country, what was bought, and the license key with its activations. Deposombe uses them to deliver and restore a license, answer support and handle refunds. Legal basis: the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Retention: kept while the license exists, so a lost key can be restored. Polar keeps its own records for as long as tax law requires.

Security

Every connection Rapidfire makes is encrypted (HTTPS). The license key and the AI assistants' token are kept in the Mac's Keychain.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access to your data (Art. 15), to rectification (Art. 16), to erasure (Art. 17) and to restriction of processing (Art. 18), and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Write to support@deposombe.com. You'll get an answer within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl.

There is no automated decision-making or profiling. Neither this site nor Rapidfire is directed at children.
