This website

deposombe.com sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites: no analytics, trackers, embedded media or third-party fonts. If you switch between light and dark, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes. Deposombe has no access to these logs, and GitHub decides how long it keeps them. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Legal basis: legitimate interests in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Email to Deposombe

Mail to support@deposombe.com is forwarded by Porkbun (USA, the domain's registrar) to a Gmail mailbox (Google). Your message, address and attachments are used only to reply to you. Retention: kept as long as the conversation needs them, then deleted, or sooner if you ask. Writing is voluntary; without an email address there is no way to reply. Legal basis: legitimate interests in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access to your data (Art. 15), to rectification (Art. 16), to erasure (Art. 17) and to restriction of processing (Art. 18), and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Write to support@deposombe.com. You'll get an answer within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl.

There is no automated decision-making or profiling. This site is not directed at children.
